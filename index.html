Learning to pray

How to pray — and what you're saying

Children and new learners are taught wuḍūʾ and each position of ṣalāh step by step, with the meaning of what is recited in each one, so prayer is performed correctly and with understanding.

  • قِيَامQiyāmstanding, reciting al-Fātiḥah
  • رُكُوعRukūʿbowing
  • سُجُودSujūdprostration
  • جُلُوسJulūssitting between prostrations
  • تَشَهُّدTashahhudthe testimony
  • تَسْلِيمTaslīmclosing with salām
The Quran pathway

From first letters to university texts

Foundation – Year 2

Early Years

  • Arabic letters, sounds & vowel marks
  • Short sūrahs with their meanings
  • Wuḍūʾ and the positions of prayer

Leaves able to read joined letters and explain short sūrahs in their own words.

Years 3 – 6

Primary

  • Fluent reading from the muṣḥaf
  • The most frequent Quranic words
  • Meaning of everything recited in ṣalāh

Leaves able to pray correctly and understand what they recite.

Years 7 – 10

Middle Years

  • Foundations of naḥw (grammar)
  • Three-letter roots & verb patterns
  • Guided translation of sūrahs

Leaves able to translate a simple verse with a dictionary.

Years 11 – 12

Senior

  • Full iʿrāb (grammatical parsing)
  • Introduction to balāghah (rhetoric)
  • Core concepts through classical tafsīr

Leaves able to parse a verse and justify every case ending.

University & adults

Higher Study

  • Classical Arabic & Islamic Studies support
  • Working with primary sources
  • Adult beginners welcome

Leaves able to read and cite classical texts independently.

How it works

Getting started

  1. Free assessment

    A relaxed 20-minute session to see where your child is — in Quran, school subjects, or both.

  2. A personal plan

    We agree goals together — reading fluency, learning to pray, catching up in Maths, or a strong study score.

  3. Weekly lessons

    Regular sessions with the same teacher, with short practice between lessons.

  4. Progress updates

    Parents receive a short update each month on what has been covered and what comes next.

Questions

Frequently asked

Where are you located?

We're opening soon in Melbourne's northern suburbs — Thomastown, Reservoir, Epping, South Morang, Mill Park and adjacent areas — with online lessons available anywhere.

Can my child do both Quran and school tutoring?

Yes — many families do. We build one weekly plan so the two fit together without overloading your child.

Does my child need to read Arabic to start the Quran program?

No. The Early Years and Primary stages begin with the letters themselves, and older beginners and adults follow the same foundations at a faster pace.

Do you teach how to pray?

Yes. We teach wuḍūʾ and every position of ṣalāh step by step, along with the meaning of what is recited.

Do your school lessons follow the Victorian Curriculum and VCE study designs?

Yes. Lessons follow what your child is studying at school, and VCE tutoring is built around the current study designs, SACs and exams.

Which VCE subjects do you tutor?

English, General Mathematics, Mathematical Methods, Specialist Mathematics, Biology, Chemistry and Psychology, Units 1–4.

How many lessons a week do you recommend?

Younger children usually do best with two shorter lessons a week. VCE students often take one longer session per subject, with extra sessions before SACs and exams.
